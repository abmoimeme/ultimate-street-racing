# Ultimate Street Racing

A browser street-racing game set in **Tokyo** and **Dakar**. Race, drift, jump ramps, escape the police, meet other drivers at the car meet and challenge them to online duels.

**Play:** https://abmoimeme.github.io/ultimate-street-racing/

Works on desktop (keyboard) and on phones (touch controls). Best in Chrome, Edge or Safari.

## Controls (keyboard)
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W / S or ↑ / ↓ | Accelerate / brake |
| A / D or ← / → | Steer |
| Space | Handbrake / drift |
| Shift | Nitro |
| E | Interact (events, garage, car meet) |
| F | Get out and walk / get in a vehicle |
| C | Change camera |
| R | Reset car onto the road |
| T | Quick chat (online), then 1–9 to send |
| P / Esc | Pause menu (graphics quality is here) |

## Walk and switch rides
- Stop and press **F** (or tap 🚪 on phones) to get out and walk anywhere in the city. Your car stays parked where you left it.
- Look for **🛵 RIDE** stands on the sidewalks to hop on a free sport bike, Tiak-Tiak scooter, Jakarta moto or bicycle. Press **F** next to one to ride it.
- Walk back to your car anytime and press **F** to drive it again. Riding into the garage brings your car to you.
- Walk up to other online players to use emotes (1–8), send quick chat (T) or challenge them to a race (E).

## Friends, the Lounge and rewards
- **👥 Friends:** see who is online and where (Tokyo or Dakar, driving, on foot or in the Lounge). Tap **⭐ ADD FRIEND**, then **🚀 JOIN** to jump straight next to them.
- **🛋️ The Lounge:** the Teranga Lounge (Dakar) and Neon Lounge (Tokyo) are chill zones with no racing. There's a stage, benches, a DJ booth and a showcase that spins your own car. Walk up to players to see their player card.
- **😀 Emotes:** wave, sabar dance, clap, flex, cheer, laugh, sit and pose (keys 1–8, or tap 😀).
- **👕 New looks:** mohawk, ponytail, headphones, bucket hat, helmet, gold crown, backpack, Lions scarf and hero cape.
- **💛 Best friends & 👻 appear offline:** pin up to 5 best friends at the top of your list, or switch on Appear Offline to play privately (nobody sees you in Friends or can Join you).
- **🔁 Rejoin:** if the game crashes, the Wi-Fi drops or the page reloads, tap REJOIN to go right back where you were.
- **🎨 Fit and color:** give hats their own color and adjust their height and tilt. Six free starter outfits help new players look sharp right away.
- **↩️ Sell back:** changed your mind about a car? Sell it back in the garage for 60% of the price.
- **📅 Daily streak:** play every day for a 7-day reward calendar. Day 3 unlocks headphones and day 7 the gold crown.
- **🏅 Badges:** 23 badges for racing, exploring, secrets, friends and streaks. Each one pays $250, and you can show your favorite one next to your name.

## Languages 🇬🇧 🇫🇷 🇮🇹
- Play in English, French or Italian. Tap EN / FR / IT in the top-right corner of the start screen or the pause menu. The game also starts in French or Italian if your phone is set to that language.
- Menus, buttons, tips, badges, chat messages and signs are translated. Quick chat shows up in each player's own language.

## Course Builder (🛠️)
- Build your own race right on your phone. Tap to place a start line, up to 12 checkpoints, cones, ramps, boost pads and barriers. Drag to move around, pinch or ± to zoom, and use Undo if you make a mistake.
- Name it by picking two words (like "Baobab Jumps"), choose 1–3 laps, then test-drive it.
- Publish it so every player can race it. Courses can be sorted by top rated, newest, friends or city, and each shows its record time. Rate courses you finish with 1–5 stars.
- Finishing another player's course pays $250 (once per course per day). There are new badges for publishing a course, finishing 5 courses and getting a 5-star rating.

## Avatar Studio (👕 Wardrobe)
- **Age, height and build:** child, teen, adult or elder, plus taller/shorter and slim, regular or broad.
- **Skin:** 19 skin tones from deep to light, including warm, cool and olive undertones. Skin details include freckles, vitiligo or a birthmark.
- **Face:** stubble, mustache, goatee or full beard, plus shades, round or square glasses.
- **Hair:** cornrows, box braids, afro puffs, waves, curly, long straight and receding, plus grey and white hair colors. Hats squash big hair so nothing pokes through.
- **Headwear:** hijab, turban and kippah, alongside kufi, cap, musoor and more. Headwear has its own color, size, height and tilt.
- **Clothes:** abaya, thawb, kurta, dashiki, sari, kimono, grand boubou, school uniform, tracksuit, blazer & tie, scrubs, long skirt, leggings and chinos.
- **Outer layers:** vest, denim jacket, cardigan or long coat over shirts and hoodies.
- **Assistive:** a wheelchair (with its own rolling animation), cane, hearing aid, cochlear implant, or a prosthetic leg or arm in carbon, metal or your own color.

## Pets, crates and Teranga Fest (🎁 Collection)
- **🐾 Pets:** 11 pets to collect, from a Puppy, Kitten, Chick and Bunny up to a Lion Cub, Panda, Golden Lion and Fire Bird. Your pet follows you on foot and rides on your car roof, and other players can see it.
- **🥚 Eggs:** you get eggs from daily streak days 2 and 5, as a bonus when you win a race, from crates, or by buying one for $2,000. Eggs hatch after 1.5 km of driving (walking counts triple). The hatch odds are always shown.
- **🎁 Mystery crates:** $3,000 of in-game cash per crate, never real money. They hold car paints, underglows, titles and eggs. The odds are shown, and duplicates turn into cash.
- **🏷️ Titles:** show a title like "Neon Rider" or "Legend of the Streets" on your player card.
- **🎉 Teranga Fest (Nov 11, 2026 – Jan 10, 2027):** find 10 glowing Golden Baobab Seeds in Dakar and 10 Lucky Lanterns in Tokyo. Look for the beams of light. Prizes: a Golden egg, a Golden Kufi, a Lantern Pack, festival titles, a Festival underglow and the Teranga Fest 2026 badge.

## Signing in with a player code
- Each player gets a **player code** from the game owner, like `LION-1234`, printed on a player card.
- Scan the QR code on the card, or open the game and type the code. You only need to do it once on each phone or computer.
- The first time, pick a **driver name**. That's the name other players see, so don't use your full real name.
- Your cars, cash, character and progress are saved to your code, so you keep them on every device.
- Keep your code secret. Anyone with your code can play as you.
- No email, phone number or real name is ever asked for. Rude driver names are blocked.
- **Guests** can play on one device without a code, but online play needs a player code.

## Safe online play
- Online chat only uses friendly quick messages and emojis (like "🔥 Nice drift!", "🏁 Race me?" or "👋 Nanga def!"). Nobody can type free text to other players.
- Other players only ever see your gamer tag, level, car, look and the badge you choose to show.
- Items can't be traded and nothing is ever bought with real money.

## For testers
- Online play and leaderboards connect automatically after you sign in. Other testers in the same city appear on your map.
- If the game runs slowly, open the pause menu and set **Graphics** to Medium or Low.
- Please send feedback with your device, browser and what you were doing when something went wrong.
